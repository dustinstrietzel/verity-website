How it works

Most jobs start with a single email.

  1. Send your request

    Email PDFs with the details below. We'll reply to confirm acceptance, timeline, and any travel fee before work begins. Prefer paper? Document pickup is available within our service area.

  2. We attempt service

    Attempts at varied times, including evenings and weekends, with an update after each one.

  3. Get your affidavit

    Once served, you receive a notarized affidavit of service by email, ready to file. Originals mailed on request.

What to include in your email

  • Documents to be served (PDF)
  • Name of the person or business to serve
  • Service address(es)
  • Service level: routine, rush, or priority
  • Case number and court, if available
  • Any deadline or hearing date
  • Description or photo of the person, if available
  • Best times to find them, if known

About Verity

Verity Process Service is an independent, locally owned process serving company based in Littleton, Colorado. We serve attorneys, property managers, businesses, and individuals across Denver's south metro.

Our focus is simple: careful, professional service done on time, with clear communication and accurate, court-ready documentation.

Verity Process Service is not a law firm and cannot provide legal advice.
